## Content Framework

# THE AMO AI VELOCITY SYSTEM

**Build AI in 8 Weeks. Not 8 Months.**  
A structured 4-phase framework designed to move from business analysis to production-ready AI — fast.

---

## 🚀 PHASE 1 — WEEK 1

### AI & Business Systems Audit

**What This Phase Does**  
We conduct a complete business and AI opportunity analysis to identify where intelligence, automation, and agents will create measurable impact.

**What We Analyze**
- Revenue model & growth levers
- Customer journey & friction points
- Sales & support workflows
- Internal operational bottlenecks
- Data quality & integration readiness

**Outcome**  
A prioritized AI roadmap aligned to ROI — not assumptions.

**Deliverables**
- AI Opportunity Audit Report
- Feature Prioritization Matrix (Impact vs Effort)
- Automation & Agent Blueprint
- Integration Architecture Overview
- 8-Week Execution Plan

---

## ⚡ PHASE 2 — WEEKS 2–3

### System Design & Intelligence Architecture

**What This Phase Does**  
We translate strategy into structured system logic, UX flows, and AI behavior design.

**What We Build**
- UX flows & interaction logic
- Data models & permission layers
- AI behavior framework (prompts, retrieval, tools)
- Technical architecture blueprint

**Outcome**  
A validated system blueprint ready for production build.

**Deliverables**
- Wireframes & interaction maps
- AI logic architecture
- Data & integration schema
- Sprint implementation plan

---

## 🏗 PHASE 3 — WEEKS 4–7

### Build, Integrate & Deploy Intelligence

**What This Phase Does**  
We develop production-grade AI systems in structured weekly sprints.

**What We Implement**
- RAG systems & knowledge retrieval
- AI agents with tool execution
- CRM & WhatsApp automation sync
- Workflow orchestration
- Admin dashboard & analytics layer

**Outcome**  
Fully functional AI integrated into your real systems — not a prototype.

**Deliverables**
- Live working AI agents
- Connected CRM & automation flows
- Production-ready infrastructure
- Weekly demo releases

---

## 🚀 PHASE 4 — WEEK 8 + ONGOING

### Launch, Optimize & Scale

**What This Phase Does**  
We deploy, enable your team, and continuously optimize using real usage data.

**What We Execute**
- QA & launch checklist
- Monitoring & observability setup
- Team training & documentation
- Performance tuning
- Continuous AI improvement roadmap

**Outcome**  
AI running in production with measurable performance metrics.

**Deliverables**
- Live production deployment
- Monitoring dashboards
- Optimization strategy
- Handoff + support framework
